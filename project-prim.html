Design of a Wind Turbine

Context

Group project aiming to design, size, and tolerance a vertical-axis wind turbine according to a given set of technical specifications. The work covered mechanical sizing, an original blade-folding mechanism designed by the team, and geometric tolerancing of the system.

Skills Acquired

· Mechanical sizing and engineering calculations from a technical specification
· Mechanism design (blade-folding strategy)
· Geometric dimensioning and tolerancing
· 3D CAD modeling and assembly design
· Teamwork and task coordination within a 3-person team

Software Used

· Excel (sizing calculations)
· CATIA V6 (3D design and tolerancing)
