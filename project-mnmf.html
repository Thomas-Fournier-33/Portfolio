Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) of Arterial Blood Flow

Context

Group project as part of the MNMF module (Numerical Methods in Fluid Mechanics), studying the impact of arterial diseases — atherosclerosis and aneurysms — on blood flow, mainly in the abdominal aorta, using CFD.

Skills Acquired

· CAD modeling for arterial geometries
· CFD simulation setup and mesh/flow convergence iteration
· Analysis of downstream flow quality (post-stenosis and post-aneurysm)
· Teamwork and task coordination within a 3-person team
· Data interpretation applied to a biomedical/fluid mechanics context

Software Used

· 3DEXPERIENCE
· Ansys Fluent
