Simulation of a Monoski Bullet System

Context

Project carried out in a team of three as part of the DYMU module (Multibody Dynamics), aiming to optimize a monoski bullet system subjected to realistic conditions such as bumps and resonance frequency excitation. The spring and joints were sized accordingly, and the whole simulation was developed in Adams and independently reproduced in Python to compare and validate both numerical approaches.

Skills Acquired

· Multibody dynamics modeling and simulation
· Dynamic system sizing (spring stiffness, joints)
· Vibration and resonance analysis
· Numerical programming and cross-validation of simulation results
· Teamwork within a 3-person team

Software Used

· Adams (multibody simulation)
· Python (independent numerical model for comparison)
